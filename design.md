# CALLOUT interface system

CALLOUT is a research directory with a persistent workspace shell. Every route shares this system.

- Genre: modern-minimal, with pixel illustration as a restrained brand accent.
- App family: Workbench. Persistent left navigation, compact page headers, searchable records, and contextual detail pages.
- Content family: Long Document. Reading-width prose with a secondary contents rail.
- Existing mint, paper, ink, muted, rule and focus tokens in `tokens.css` are authoritative.
- Space Grotesk is the interface and heading face. Tiny5 is reserved for the wordmark and Order artwork.
- Dense information uses aligned rows and tabular numbers. Do not decorate missing data as metrics.
- Controls use a 44 px minimum target, visible labels, immediate focus rings and consistent borders.
- Page entrance uses a short opacity/translation transition. Reduced motion removes spatial motion. No scroll reveals or looping decoration.
- The shell becomes a labeled horizontal navigation on tablets and a disclosure menu on phones.
- No chapter-number sections, full-screen hero, inactive purchase buttons or fictional records in the public interface.
- Unconfigured financial or social actions are omitted. Source caveats remain visible wherever source statistics appear.
